About Crystal Training Academy

Crystal Training Academy is a CTEVT-affiliated hospitality training institute under Trade Tower Group, operating directly inside Hotel Crystal Pashupati. We provide industry-focused education in an authentic, luxury hotel setting, combining classroom theory with hands-on practical experience.

  • Real-world Training inside Hotel Crystal Pashupati
  • Experienced Industry Trainers
  • CTEVT Affiliated Hospitality Courses
  • Guaranteed Internship & Placement Support
